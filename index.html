Contact Sheet

Cull a shoot on 6×6 contact sheets with a red grease pencil. Everything stays on this device: nothing is uploaded, and after the first visit the app works offline.

Open

Open folder for a folder that holds one shoot, such as on an SD card or a hard drive. Open photos to select just one shoot's files from a folder that holds more, such as the tablet's camera folder: in the Android file picker, long-press the first file, then tap the others. JPEGs, Sony ARW and Fujifilm RAF are read. A RAW and a JPEG with the same name are one frame. Frames are sorted by capture time. Use Chrome or Edge.

Rated only / All

Shows only frames rated in camera, or every frame. It starts on rated frames when there are any. Rated frames have gold corner marks. In Rated only, a number such as +4 between frames counts the unrated frames hidden there.

Pick and mark

  • Tap a frame to pick it (it gets a red outline). Tap again to unpick. With a finger this always works; with a pen or mouse, choose Select first.
  • Double-tap a frame to open it large in the loupe.
  • Pen draws, pressure sensitive. To write notes, zoom in first: the line gets finer the further you zoom, so small writing stays readable. Box drags a box; drag its edge to move it or a corner to resize it. Eraser, or the pen's eraser button, removes a whole mark.
  • Fingers never draw, so a resting palm is ignored.
  • Undo takes back the last change on this sheet. Clear removes its drawings; picks stay.
  • Marks save by themselves. Opening the same photos again brings them back.

Zoom and magnify

  • Pinch with two fingers or turn the mouse wheel to zoom the sheet; drag to move around. Fit shows the whole sheet again.
  • Magnifier: hold a hovering pen still over a frame for a moment and a lens appears just above the tip, magnifying what is under it. It follows the pen until you touch down or lift the pen away. The button turns it off.
  • In the loupe: double-tap or 100% to check focus, drag to pan, ‹ › for the next frame. RAW-only frames show the preview stored in the RAW, which on some cameras is only 1616 px wide.
  • ▴ hides most buttons for a bigger sheet.

Export

  • Export saves each sheet as a JPEG plus one PDF, and a list of your picks. File names carry the export date and time, so earlier exports are kept.
  • After Open folder, these go into a Picks folder inside it, and the picked JPEGs are copied there too. RAWs are not copied; they stay where they are. After Open photos, everything goes to Downloads and no photos are copied.
  • On a tablet, a picked frame can be shared from the loupe, for example to an editing app.

Move a cull to another device

Export marks saves your picks and drawings as a small file. On the other device, open the same photos, then Import marks.

What it never does

It never changes, moves, renames or deletes your photos; it only reads them and adds files to Picks. It doesn't develop RAWs, and other camera RAW formats are not read.
